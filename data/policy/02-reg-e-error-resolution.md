# Policy: Electronic Fund Transfer Error Resolution (Regulation E)

**Applies to:** Complaints alleging an unauthorized electronic transaction, a duplicate electronic payment, or an incorrect amount debited electronically (ACH, debit card, mobile app payment).

## Requirements
- Customer must notify us within **60 days** of the statement reflecting the error.
- We must investigate and resolve within **10 business days** of notice (extendable to **45 days** if we provisionally credit the disputed amount within the original 10 days and notify the customer in writing).
- If we determine no error occurred, we must provide a written explanation and the customer may request copies of documents relied upon.
- For new accounts (open less than 30 days), the investigation period extends to 20 business days, with up to 90 days total if provisional credit is given.

## What an analyst or agent may say
- May confirm whether a transaction appears duplicated or unauthorized based on the transaction log.
- May state the applicable investigation timeline and whether provisional credit has been or will be issued.
- May NOT promise a permanent credit before the investigation is complete — only a *provisional* credit, clearly labeled as such, is permitted within the timeline above.

## Escalation trigger
Any complaint alleging the error was never investigated within the required timeline, or alleging a pattern of similar errors, should be flagged for supervisor review — this is both a customer-harm and a regulatory-timeliness risk.

## Remedy authority
Front-line staff may issue **provisional credit** up to $100 without supervisor approval while an investigation is pending; amounts above $100, or any *final* credit determination, require supervisor sign-off.

*Source: Electronic Fund Transfer Act, 15 U.S.C. § 1693 et seq.; Regulation E, 12 C.F.R. Part 1005. Generic, illustrative summary for internal training/demo purposes, not legal advice.*
