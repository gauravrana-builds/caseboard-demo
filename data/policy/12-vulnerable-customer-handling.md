# Internal Policy: Vulnerable Customer Handling

**Applies to:** Complaints where the customer discloses or where context indicates elderly age, cognitive or physical disability, limited English proficiency, military service with an active hardship (deployment, PCS move), or other circumstances that may affect their ability to engage with standard processes.

## What changes for a vulnerable customer
- **Response complexity:** use shorter sentences, avoid conditional/multi-step instructions where possible, and offer a phone follow-up option in addition to the written response.
- **Timeline sensitivity:** prioritize these cases for faster turnaround within the standard 15-day window rather than defaulting to the maximum allowed time.
- **Language access:** if limited English proficiency is indicated, flag for translated response or interpreter-assisted follow-up rather than sending a standard English-only response.
- **Military-specific:** the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act (SCRA) may provide interest-rate caps and other protections for active-duty servicemembers — any complaint referencing active military status and financial hardship should be flagged for SCRA eligibility review, not handled as a standard hardship request.

## What does NOT change
- Remedy authority thresholds (fee waiver amounts, etc.) are not expanded based on vulnerability status alone — a vulnerability flag changes *how* we communicate and *how quickly* we act, not what we are authorized to approve.
- Escalation criteria from policy 09 still apply independently — a vulnerable customer whose complaint also mentions an attorney still requires mandatory escalation.

## What an analyst or agent may say
- May offer a phone follow-up or translated response as a normal accommodation.
- May NOT make assumptions about a customer's capacity or needs beyond what they have actually disclosed — do not self-diagnose a vulnerability the customer hasn't indicated.

*This is an internal, generic policy created for a training/demonstration environment and does not reflect any specific company's actual policy.*
