# Internal Policy: Hardship and Payment Deferral Options

**Applies to:** Complaints where a customer states they are struggling to make payments, references job loss, medical emergency, or similar hardship, or explicitly requests a payment plan or deferral.

## Available options (in order of customer impact, least to most significant)
1. **Payment date shift** — move the due date within the same billing cycle by up to 10 days, no fee, available once per 12-month period, analyst may approve directly.
2. **Reduced-payment plan** — temporarily reduce the monthly payment for up to 3 months, extending the loan term accordingly. Requires supervisor approval and a documented hardship reason.
3. **Interest-only or fee-deferral period** — defer principal payments for up to 2 months in cases of documented significant hardship (job loss, medical emergency, natural disaster). Requires supervisor approval.
4. **Full forbearance** — pause all payments for up to 60 days. Reserved for severe, documented hardship. Requires manager approval.

## Eligibility
- Account must be no more than 60 days past due at time of request.
- Customer must state a reason for hardship (does not need to be independently verified for options 1-2; options 3-4 require the customer to describe the nature of the hardship, though no documentation/proof is required at this stage).

## What an analyst or agent may say
- May offer option 1 directly, with no further approval needed.
- May describe options 2-4 as "available, pending supervisor review" — may NOT confirm a plan is approved until supervisor sign-off is actually logged.
- May NOT suggest an account will be reported as current to credit bureaus during a reduced-payment or forbearance period unless that is actually how the specific plan is structured — this must be confirmed, not assumed.

## Escalation trigger
A customer who has already received a hardship accommodation and is requesting a second one within the same 12-month period, or who indicates the hardship accommodation itself failed to resolve their situation, should be flagged for supervisor review rather than a standard offer.

*This is an internal, generic policy created for a training/demonstration environment and does not reflect any specific company's actual policy.*
