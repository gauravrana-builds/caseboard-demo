# Internal Policy: Response Tone and Format Requirements

**Applies to:** The structure and formatting of every drafted customer response.

## Required structure
1. **Opening acknowledgment** — brief, direct reference to the complaint received (no generic "thank you for contacting us" filler that doesn't reference the specific issue).
2. **What we found** — plain-language summary of what was investigated, referencing specific dates/amounts from the account where relevant.
3. **Outcome/resolution** — the specific remedy (if any), or a clear explanation of why no remedy applies.
4. **Next steps** — what, if anything, the customer needs to do, and how to follow up.
5. **Case reference number** — always included for any further correspondence.

## Tone guidelines
- Second person, direct address ("your account," "you contacted us about").
- Plain language — avoid internal terms (e.g., say "late fee" not "DQ assessment").
- Length: a routine single-issue complaint response should be 100–200 words; a multi-allegation complaint may run longer, but every allegation must still be addressed individually and concisely.
- No marketing language, no cross-sell/upsell content of any kind in a complaint response.

## Formatting
- Use short paragraphs (2-4 sentences). Avoid dense single-paragraph responses.
- When addressing multiple allegations, consider using a brief list or clearly separated paragraphs, one per allegation, rather than blending them together.

*This is an internal, generic policy created for a training/demonstration environment and does not reflect any specific company's actual policy.*
