# Internal Policy: Fee Waiver and Goodwill Credit Authority

**Applies to:** Complaints where account/transaction data confirms a fee was charged in error, or where a goodwill gesture is being considered for a confirmed service failure.

## Authority thresholds
| Fee/credit amount | Who may approve |
|---|---|
| $0 – $50 | Complaints analyst, no additional sign-off required |
| $50.01 – $200 | Complaints analyst with supervisor co-sign |
| $200.01+ | Supervisor or manager approval required; analyst may not approve independently |

## When a waiver is appropriate
- The transaction history **confirms** the fee was charged in error (e.g., payment was received before the due date but a late fee was still assessed).
- The fee was charged due to a documented system or processing error.
- A documented goodwill exception for a first-time, low-dollar fee on an otherwise good-standing account (use sparingly, document the rationale).

## When a waiver is NOT appropriate
- The transaction history confirms the fee was correctly charged (no error occurred) — the response should explain the charge, not waive it.
- The complaint's underlying fact could not be verified (missing/incomplete data) — route to manual review rather than waiving preemptively.
- The complaint involves a dispute of right vs. convenience (e.g., customer disagrees with a disclosed, legitimate fee) — explain the fee and its basis; do not waive solely to end the complaint.

## What an analyst or agent may say
- May state the exact dollar amount being waived and the specific reason (tied to the confirmed error).
- May NOT waive a fee "as a one-time courtesy" without a documented rationale on file — every waiver must cite either a confirmed error or an explicit goodwill justification.

## Documentation requirement
Every waiver, regardless of amount, must be logged with: the confirming evidence (which transaction record), the dollar amount, and the approving party.

*This is an internal, generic policy created for a training/demonstration environment and does not reflect any specific company's actual policy.*
