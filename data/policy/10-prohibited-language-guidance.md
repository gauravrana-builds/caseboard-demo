# Internal Policy: Prohibited Language in Customer Responses

**Applies to:** Every drafted response, regardless of complaint type.

## Never say
- **Fault/liability admissions beyond confirmed fact:** "We sincerely apologize for our mistake," "this was entirely our fault," "we take full responsibility." Instead, state the confirmed fact plainly: "our records confirm this fee was applied in error."
- **Guarantees about the future:** "this will never happen again," "you will not be charged this fee going forward" (unless a specific, documented account change actually prevents it).
- **Legal conclusions:** "this does not violate any regulation," "you have no legal basis for this complaint" — never characterize legal compliance or a customer's legal rights.
- **Promises outside documented authority:** any remedy, waiver, or account change that has not actually been approved per the relevant policy (fee waiver thresholds, hardship approval, etc.).
- **Discouraging further action:** "there's no need to contact anyone else about this," "this complaint can be considered closed on all fronts" — never imply a customer should not pursue other channels (regulator complaint, legal counsel) if they choose to.

## Always include
- A clear statement of what was investigated and found.
- The specific outcome (approved remedy, explanation of why no remedy applies, or next steps if still pending).
- A case or reference number for any follow-up.

## Tone requirements
- Professional, respectful, and plain-language — avoid legal or technical jargon without explanation.
- Acknowledge customer frustration without being performative ("we understand this has been frustrating" is fine; excessive apology is not).

*This is an internal, generic policy created for a training/demonstration environment and does not reflect any specific company's actual policy.*
